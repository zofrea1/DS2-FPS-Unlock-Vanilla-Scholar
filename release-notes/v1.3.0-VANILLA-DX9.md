# Dark Souls II (Vanilla), FPS Unlock v1.3.0

High-FPS unlock with gameplay physics fixes. Files: `xinput1_3.dll` and `DS2-FPS-Unlock.ini`, next to `DarkSoulsII.exe`. This is the 32-bit DirectX 9 build for the original Dark Souls II; Scholar of the First Sin uses the Scholar (DX11) release.

## New in 1.3.0
- **Motion blur without ghosting** (`FixMotionBlur`, on by default). The original game stretches each frame's blur by the frame rate it measures, so at 240 FPS every frame was blurred over four frames' worth of motion, leaving ghost trails behind moving things and when turning the camera. The frame rate the blur uses is now capped at 60, so each frame blurs its own motion as at 60 FPS. Both motion blur and camera motion blur can stay on.
- **Skips the start-up notice and logos** (`SkipIntro`, on by default). The title screen opens at once, the way it does when you quit to the title from the game; nothing is sped up. `IntroSkip` also works as the key's name. Works with or without `FPSUnlock`.

## Install
Copy both files next to the game exe. Upgrading from 1.2.0: replace `xinput1_3.dll`; your INI keeps working, and both new switches are on even when it does not list them. `FPSUnlock = false` leaves the frame rate and gameplay unchanged.

## Known limits
The unlocked picture is fullscreen only; in windowed mode the counter can run ahead of the image, which stays at 60. Lighting can flicker at aspect ratios far from 16:9 (seen at 9:16 with the display in portrait; 16:10 does not flicker). Most of the HUD and the FMV videos are not Vert+: they stay the shape the game draws them. Cloth speed is corrected, but the cloth simulation is not identical to 60 FPS. Online play against 60 FPS players seems fine, but it is not guaranteed to match. See the README. Send `DS2-FPS-Unlock.log` with bug reports.

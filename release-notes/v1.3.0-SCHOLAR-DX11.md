# Dark Souls II: Scholar of the First Sin, FPS Unlock v1.3.0

High-FPS unlock with gameplay physics fixes. Files: `xinput1_3.dll` and `DS2-FPS-Unlock.ini`, next to `DarkSoulsII.exe`. This is the 64-bit Scholar of the First Sin build; the original Dark Souls II uses the Vanilla (DX9) release.

## New in 1.3.0
- **Skips the start-up notice and logos** (`SkipIntro`, on by default). The title screen opens at once, the way it does when you quit to the title from the game; nothing is sped up. `IntroSkip` also works as the key's name. Works with or without `FPSUnlock`.
- `FixMotionBlur` (the original game's motion blur ghosting) changes nothing in Scholar of the First Sin: its blur already follows the frame time.

## Install
Copy both files next to the game exe. Upgrading from 1.2.0: replace `xinput1_3.dll`; your INI keeps working, and the new switch is on even when it does not list it. `FPSUnlock = false` leaves the frame rate and gameplay unchanged.

## Known limits
Most of the HUD and the FMV videos are not Vert+: they stay the shape the game draws them. Wider screens (21:9) also fill, but that has not been checked in game. Cloth speed is corrected, but the cloth simulation is not identical to 60 FPS. Online play against 60 FPS players seems fine, but it is not guaranteed to match. See the README. Send `DS2-FPS-Unlock.log` with bug reports.
